About Us

ACTIVA (Actuarial and Financial Consulting) is a student-led consultancy specializing in financial and actuarial advisory services. Our team consists of actuarial students with strong analytical skills in risk assessment, financial modeling, and economic analysis.

We provide data-driven solutions to businesses, organizations, and institutions seeking to optimize their financial and actuarial strategies while actively contributing to Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) 8: Decent Work and Economic Growth by promoting responsible financial planning and risk management.

Activa is committed to delivering accurate analyses, strategic solutions, and customized approaches tailored to the challenges our partners face. Through our work, we help businesses foster economic resilience, financial stability, and sustainable progress.
